    Copy trading

    Copy the trades of a ABEC-FX mentor on your own MetaTrader 5 account, using your own lot size.

    Loading...

    How it works. You choose one master. When they open a trade, your MT5 opens the same trade with your lot size and their stop loss and take profit. When they move the stop loss or take profit, or close the trade, your copy does the same.

    Good to know. Only trades the master opens after you start following are copied, and only while their MT5 is online. A copy is only opened within a minute of the master's trade, and only once it has a stop loss. Your MT5 must be open and linked (Bot trading, Account link).

    Choose a master

    A master's results are on their own account and in their own currency. They show the past and do not promise anything about the future.

    The master's own lot size is never used.

    Counted in Lagos time.

      Run your connector on the ABEC-FX VPS

      Instead of keeping your own computer on, you can ask for your MT5 connector to run on the ABEC-FX VPS. The website never asks for a password. If your request is approved, someone from ABEC-FX will contact you to arrange access privately, and your trading password goes only into the terminal on the VPS.       Copy trading follows someone else's decisions, not advice from ABEC-FX, and losses are possible. If you also use the signals bot on the same account, both can open trades. Only risk money you can afford to lose.
