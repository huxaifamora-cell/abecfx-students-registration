1. Press Link account to make your connection key.

2. Press Download connector and put the file in MT5 (File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts), then compile it in MetaEditor (F7) and restart MT5 or refresh the Navigator.

3. In MT5, go to Tools > Options > Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest and add . Make sure Algo Trading is on (green).

4. Drag ABECFX_Bot_Connector onto any chart, paste your key into InpBotKey, and press OK. Keep MT5 open (a VPS is best).
