Make quiz codes

Students cannot open the quiz until they enter a one-time code. Make codes here and give them to your students. A code opens the quiz for one student and stays open until they finish it. After that the quiz locks again, and the next attempt needs a new code.

    Site links

    These show as icons on the website (portal, register and Check ID pages) and change as soon as you press Save. Leave a box empty to hide that icon. Web addresses must start with https://

    Student community

    Students see these on the Community page of their portal, as "Open WhatsApp community" and "Open Telegram community". Paste the invite link of your group. A button only appears for a link you set; leave a box empty to hide it. Links must start with https://

    Chapter video tutorials

    Paste the YouTube address of the video for each chapter. Students see it inside that chapter of the Handbook. A chapter with no address shows "coming soon". Unlisted videos work too, but they must allow embedding.

    ID cards

    Find any student or team member and open their ID card. If a person holds more than one (Student, Alumni, Team), all of their ID cards show when you click their name. Search by name, registration number, country, state or status.

      Who signs the certificates

      Enter the registration number of the person who signs (the CEO, for example). Their name, their role and the signature on their own ID card are printed on every certificate automatically, so there is nothing to upload here. If they change the signature on their ID card, the certificates follow. The organisation name and CAC RC number print on every certificate too.

      Printed as “CAC RC: 9420199”. Type the number only.

      Certificates

      Every Certified student has one. Search by name or registration number, then download it as a picture (PNG) or a PDF.

        Team

        Each person has a role, and each role decides what they can open. The main owner (the ADMIN_PASSWORD holder) always has full access and is not listed here. An owner can make someone else an owner by giving them the Owner role; admins cannot.

        Roles and access

        Admin, Editor and Moderator are built in and cannot be changed. Create your own role and tick exactly what it may open. You can only give access that you have yourself.
